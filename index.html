Cooperation of European Union Members

See how members work together across procedures

Made by group 7

Notes

This analysis is made on a costum dataset consisting of information on Members of the European Parliament, focusing on the relationship between rapporteurs and shadow rapporteurs across Community Policies from the ninth term (2019/2024). All data is scraped directly from the Legislative Observatory

The goal is provide insights to how members work across legislative procedures and making collaborations across parties more transparent, focussing on trends within the procedures and which politicians are most influential.

Who are connected with who?

Each link represents a collaboration between two members. A thresholding based on a minimum number of collaborations between members to avoid full connectivity. The larger the node, the more procedures a member has worked on. Nodes are colored by topic. (See explainer notebook for full details and more in depth analysis and visualization)

Basic inspection

The full network contains 753 nodes with 7495 links where 1318 links more than 3 procedures. After applying a threshold based on a minimum number of procedures shared, the network degree of connectivity is 3.501, which shows a moderate level of cohesion across nodes, indicating that many members share multiple procedures.
